# Album artwork fixture

The offline Desk preview shows M83's *Hurry Up, We're Dreaming*, containing
“Midnight City.” Album information and artwork come from the
[artist's official album page](https://ilovem83.com/music/hurry-up-were-dreaming/).
The [cover image](https://ilovem83.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/CS438363-01A-BIG.jpg)
is retained as an album-art fixture; its original dimensions are 700 × 718.
Artwork rights remain with their respective owners.

The sample track length, 4:03, follows the
[artist's Bandcamp listing](https://ilovem83.bandcamp.com/album/hurry-up-were-dreaming).

`desk-120x32.png` depicts the artwork layer available with terminal graphics.
`desk-halfblocks-120x32.png` shows the actual exported cell fallback for terminals
without a graphics protocol. Both use the same native layout and source image.
